🚀 "AI-Powered Business Operations Platform" (Mini SaaS for Startups/Freelancers)

Ye ek multi-tenant SaaS hoga — matlab multiple businesses/teams isko use kar sakein, sirf ek user nahi. Ye complexity level bohot upar le jati hai:

Kya hoga isme:

🏢 Multi-tenant architecture — Har company/team apna alag workspace bana sake (jaise Slack workspaces), team members invite kar sake with roles (Admin, Member, Viewer)
👥 Team Collaboration — Real-time updates (Socket.io se) — jab ek banda task update kare, sab ko live dikhe
🤖 AI Business Assistant — Meeting notes upload karo, AI action items nikale aur automatically team members ko assign kare
📈 Smart Analytics Dashboard — AI business data (sales, tasks, time) analyze kare aur weekly insights/reports khud generate kare ("Is week productivity 15% badhi")
💳 Payment/Subscription simulation — Stripe integration (test mode) — free vs paid plan, feature-locking (ye SaaS ka core concept hai)
📧 Automated Notifications — Email/in-app alerts (deadline aane wali hai, task assign hua, etc.)
🔐 Advanced permission system — Role-based access control (RBAC) — ye interview mein bohot serious topic hai
